# Seeking Alpha API (Unofficial)

**Quotes, estimates, ratings, news and analysis as JSON**

Real-time quotes, price history, company profiles, earnings estimates, dividends, analyst ratings, news, analysis articles, earnings-call transcripts and screeners, served as JSON. Unofficial; not affiliated with Seeking Alpha. Not investment advice.

[**Try it on RapidAPI**](https://rapidapi.com/belchiorarkad-FqvHs2EDOtP/api/seeking-alpha-api) · [Telegram support: @pintoflow](https://t.me/pintoflow)

## What you get

- Real-time quotes for up to 50 symbols per call
- Charts and daily historical prices
- Company profile, estimates, dividends, splits
- Analyst ratings and sector ranking
- News, analysis, transcripts, podcasts, stock ideas
- Screeners and curated comparisons
- Search to find tickers

## Popular use cases

- Stock dashboards and watchlists
- Research and screening tools
- Financial news aggregation
- Earnings and dividend trackers

## Quick start

1. Subscribe to the free BASIC plan on [RapidAPI](https://rapidapi.com/belchiorarkad-FqvHs2EDOtP/api/seeking-alpha-api).

```bash
  --url 'https://seeking-alpha-api.p.rapidapi.com/price?symbols=AAPL,MSFT' \
  --header 'X-RapidAPI-Key: YOUR_RAPIDAPI_KEY' \

```
